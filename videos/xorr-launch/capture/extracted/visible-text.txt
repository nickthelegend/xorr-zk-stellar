XORR — Private-by-default money on Stellar.
Tagline: "Private money, made real."

WHAT IT IS
Shield public USDC into unlinkable zero-knowledge notes, pay anyone by email or handle,
bridge USDC from Ethereum, and off-ramp to real fiat. Every spend is proven in zero
knowledge and verified on-chain by a BN254 Groth16 contract on Soroban (Stellar Protocol 25).
Private, not anonymous — a holder can disclose a view key to an auditor.

NARRATIVE (60s, product-launch arc)
hook -> problem (every on-chain payment is public and traceable) -> how it works (shield
into ZK notes; only a commitment touches the chain) -> 2-3 features (pay by email/address,
bridge from Ethereum, real fiat off-ramp) -> call to action. Two halves that flow, not a
hard split: ~first half on privacy / how-it-works, then transition naturally into the
features + CTA.

SHOW (real frontend screenshots captured)
- Deposit / Shield USDC (a ZK proof binds the note; the amount never appears on-chain)
- Pay & Receive — send a private payment to an email, @handle, or any Stellar address
- Bridge to xUSDC — bring USDC from Ethereum (Sepolia) into private xUSDC with a Groth16 proof
- Off-ramp — a real SEP-24 withdrawal, Stellar -> fiat, settled on-chain
- Money market (bonus) — supply/borrow, live oracle
- How it works: the ZK note scheme — commitment = Poseidon(amount, pk, blinding); nullifier
  revealed on spend; the on-chain pairing check gates every state change.
